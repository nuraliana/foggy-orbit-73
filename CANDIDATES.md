# Candidates — Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review

Scoring: /25 total, 5 criteria x /5 — (1) reads clearly as a one-line Earth review by an alien tourist, (2) humour from the alien view of ordinary Earth life, (3) originality, (4) sounds like a real tourist review (verdict / rating / closing line), (5) density, punch lands at the end. All candidates are a single line, ≤200 characters.

## 1. Food portrait — 20/25 (132 chars)

> The locals photograph their food before eating it, as if dinner must pose for evidence. 3/5 — I watched mine go cold for a portrait.

- (1) Premise 5/5 — alien observer ("locals"), clearly Earth tourism.
- (2) Humour 4/5 — the joke grows from the alien reading of a normal habit.
- (3) Originality 4/5 — food-photography angle, not in the crowded field.
- (4) Review voice 4/5 — rating + closing verdict present.
- (5) Density 3/5 — ending is charming but soft; "for a portrait" lands gently.

## 2. Five minutes away — 20/25 (126 chars)

> Locals say "five minutes away" while still at home; distance here is measured in lies. 2/5, arrived eventually, trust nothing.

- (1) Premise 5/5 — alien reporting a local custom.
- (2) Humour 4/5 — "measured in lies" is an alien-unit joke.
- (3) Originality 4/5 — fresh angle, not in the crowded field.
- (4) Review voice 4/5 — rating + verdict present.
- (5) Density 3/5 — "trust nothing" is a decent closer, slightly abrupt.

## 3. Tiny folding roofs (WINNER) — 24/25 (118 chars)

> They carry tiny folding roofs for when the sky leaks, then lose them immediately. 4/5 — the rain, at least, is honest.

- (1) Premise 5/5 — unmistakably an alien describing an umbrella without naming it.
- (2) Humour 5/5 — the laugh comes from the alien framing itself ("sky leaks", "tiny folding roofs"), not a pasted joke.
- (3) Originality 5/5 — umbrella-as-roof reframing; no other field angle does this.
- (4) Review voice 4/5 — rating + closing verdict present.
- (5) Density 5/5 — every word works; the punch ("the rain, at least, is honest") lands at the very end and flips the complaint onto the humans.

## 4. Paid to be frightened — 23/25 (126 chars)

## Selected entry
